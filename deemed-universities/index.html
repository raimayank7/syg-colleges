Deemed to be universities in India

An institution declared by the central government, on the advice of the UGC, to be deemed a university under Section 3 of the UGC Act. The UGC website lists 164 as on 11 November 2026.

A "deemed to be university" is an institution of higher education that is not created as a university by an Act, but is declared by the central government, on the advice of the UGC, to have the status of one under Section 3 of the UGC Act. It can then award its own degrees.

Some are public institutions and some are private. UGC rules ask them to use the words "deemed to be university" and not plain "university" in their name.

Where they are

The 164 entries are spread over 24 of the 34 states and union territories in the UGC lists. The most are in Tamil Nadu (36), Maharashtra (25), Karnataka (18), Delhi (11), Uttar Pradesh (10). 6 states and union territories have only one each. 10 states and union territories have none on this list.

Using this list

The table is sorted by state, then by name. Type a town or part of a name in the box to narrow it. The "UGC status" column is explained under the table. If an institution tells you it is one of these and you cannot find it here, look for it on ugc.gov.in before you believe it; names that sound alike are common. To see every kind of university in one state together, go to universities by state.

What "UGC status" means

  • 2(f): the university is on the UGC's list under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act.
  • 12B: it has also been declared fit to receive central grants under Section 12B.
  • Section-III: it is a deemed to be university, declared under Section 3 of the Act.

These are the UGC's own labels, shown as on its website. They say that the university is recognised. They do not rate its quality.

How many deemed to be universities are there in India?

The UGC website lists 164 as on 11 November 2026.

Which state has the most?

Tamil Nadu (36), then Maharashtra (25), then Karnataka (18), among the 24 states and union territories that have at least one.
