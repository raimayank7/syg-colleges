National institutes on the UGC list

IITs, NITs, AIIMS and similar institutions created by their own Acts of Parliament. The UGC website lists some of them; it is not a complete list of such institutes. The UGC website lists 53 as on 11 November 2026.

These are institutions such as the Indian Institutes of Technology, National Institutes of Technology and AIIMS. Each is created by an Act of Parliament and awards its own degrees. The UGC website shows them in a separate group, and shows only some of them, so an institute missing from this page is not thereby unrecognised.

Where they are

The 53 entries are spread over 31 of the 34 states and union territories in the UGC lists. The most are in Tamil Nadu (4), Delhi (3), Punjab (3), Andhra Pradesh (2), Assam (2). 13 states and union territories have only one each. 3 states and union territories have none on this list.

Using this list

The table is sorted by state, then by name. Type a town or part of a name in the box to narrow it. The "UGC status" column is explained under the table. This group is not complete on the UGC website, so an institute that is missing here may still be genuine. Check it on the website of the ministry it comes under, and in the Act that set it up. To see every kind of university in one state together, go to universities by state.

What "UGC status" means

  • 2(f): the university is on the UGC's list under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act.
  • 12B: it has also been declared fit to receive central grants under Section 12B.
  • Section-III: it is a deemed to be university, declared under Section 3 of the Act.

These are the UGC's own labels, shown as on its website. They say that the university is recognised. They do not rate its quality.

Questions people ask

How many national institutes on the ugc list are there in India?

The UGC website lists 53 as on 11 November 2026.

Which state has the most?

Tamil Nadu (4), then Delhi (3), then Punjab (3), among the 31 states and union territories that have at least one.

Source and date. The lists are those published on the website of the University Grants Commission (ugc.gov.in), read on 11 November 2026. A university can be added, renamed or removed at any time. Before you take admission or pay a fee, check the institution yourself on ugc.gov.in. This is not a government site.
