State private universities in India

Set up by an Act of a state legislature and run by a sponsoring body such as a society, trust or company. The UGC website lists 560 as on 11 November 2026.

A private university is also created by an Act of a state legislature, but it is set up and run by a sponsoring body such as a society, a trust or a company. It awards its own degrees. Under UGC rules a private university works within its own state and cannot affiliate colleges.

Fees and quality vary widely. Check the UGC status, the approvals for your course and the accreditation before you decide.

Where they are

The 560 entries are spread over 27 of the 34 states and union territories in the UGC lists. The most are in Gujarat (66), Madhya Pradesh (54), Uttar Pradesh (54), Rajasthan (53), Maharashtra (40). 2 states and union territories have only one each. 7 states and union territories have none on this list.

Using this list

The table is sorted by state, then by name. Type a town or part of a name in the box to narrow it. The "UGC status" column is explained under the table. If an institution tells you it is one of these and you cannot find it here, look for it on ugc.gov.in before you believe it; names that sound alike are common. To see every kind of university in one state together, go to universities by state.

What "UGC status" means

  • 2(f): the university is on the UGC's list under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act.
  • 12B: it has also been declared fit to receive central grants under Section 12B.
  • Section-III: it is a deemed to be university, declared under Section 3 of the Act.

These are the UGC's own labels, shown as on its website. They say that the university is recognised. They do not rate its quality.
