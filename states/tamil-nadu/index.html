What "UGC status" means

  • 2(f): the university is on the UGC's list under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act.
  • 12B: it has also been declared fit to receive central grants under Section 12B.
  • Section-III: it is a deemed to be university, declared under Section 3 of the Act.

These are the UGC's own labels, shown as on its website. They say that the university is recognised. They do not rate its quality.

Questions people ask

How many universities are there in Tamil Nadu?

The UGC website lists 73 universities and similar institutions in Tamil Nadu as on 11 November 2026: 2 central universities, 22 state universities, 9 state private universities, 36 deemed to be universities, 4 national institutes on the ugc list.

How do I check if a university in Tamil Nadu is recognised?

Look for its name in the UGC list on this page or on ugc.gov.in. Also check that it is not on the UGC list of fake universities.

Which are the central universities in Tamil Nadu?

Central University of Tamil Nadu; Indian Maritime University.

Source and date. The lists are those published on the website of the University Grants Commission (ugc.gov.in), read on 11 November 2026. A university can be added, renamed or removed at any time.
