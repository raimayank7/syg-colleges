What "UGC status" means

  • 2(f): the university is on the UGC's list under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act.
  • 12B: it has also been declared fit to receive central grants under Section 12B.
  • Section-III: it is a deemed to be university, declared under Section 3 of the Act.

These are the UGC's own labels, shown as on its website. They say that the university is recognised. They do not rate its quality.

How many universities are there in Maharashtra?

The UGC website lists 100 universities and similar institutions in Maharashtra as on 11 November 2026: 1 central university, 32 state universities, 40 state private universities, 25 deemed to be universities, 2 national institutes on the ugc list.

How do I check if a university in Maharashtra is recognised?

Look for its name in the UGC list on this page or on ugc.gov.in. Also check that it is not on the UGC list of fake universities.
